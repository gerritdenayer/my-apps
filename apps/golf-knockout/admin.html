Tournament bracket

Click the player who won each match to send them through. In the first round you can drag a player (use the ☰ handle) and drop them onto another slot to rearrange the draw. The number on each match card is the net strokes the higher handicapper receives.

Add a player

Playing Handicap drives the Net Matchplay strokes. When a draw exists, new players are dropped into the first empty slot automatically. Use the slot picker above to move a player to a specific spot. Changing the draw clears recorded results.

Create the draw

Build the knockout from your current players. Pick how many rounds you want, then choose a draw type. Top seeds get a bye if there are empty slots. The bracket appears on the Bracket tab.

Seeded draw Lowest playing handicap is seed 1. Strong players are kept apart until late rounds.
Random draw Names drawn out of the hat. Pure luck of the draw.

Import players (paste from Excel)

Copy two columns from Excel (Name and exact handicap / index) and paste them below, one player per row. The playing handicap is set to the rounded index; adjust it per player afterwards if needed.

Tournament title

This title appears at the top of the Bracket and Players tabs, and on the printed sheet.

Color scheme

Pick a scheme to match your website or club branding. It restyles the whole app and is saved automatically.

Round deadlines

Set the date each round should be finished by. These show under the round name on the bracket. Deadlines appear once a draw exists.

Publish to web (JSONBin.io)

Connect to your free JSONBin.io account so visitors to your website can see the live bracket.

Backup & restore

All data lives only in this browser on your iMac. Export a backup regularly, and use it to restore or move to another computer.

Reset

Delete the current draw, or wipe everything. Make a backup first.
